* Design views anchor the design to the top-left corner of the pane instead of centring it, in Fit and at an absolute zoom alike, for the WinUI, WPF, WinForms, GTK and MewUI designers. Wheel zoom keeps the point under the cursor in place, and the surface no longer toggles its scrollbars back and forth while the pane is resized.
* The Microsoft WinUI design host no longer loops forever on pages that contain host-defined types (the real WinUI Gallery's SemanticZoomPage hung the host until it was killed), and it parses deeply nested XAML on a 64 MB stack so such pages no longer die with a stack overflow.
* An installed OpenDevelop ships `OpenDevelop.host-assemblies.txt` at its application root, so an add-in built with `PackOdAddin` against an installed IDE is trimmed of the host's assemblies instead of carrying a second copy (CoreWF's `.odaddin` was 435 MB).
