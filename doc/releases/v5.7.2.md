* The GTK designer covers much more of GTK 4 and libadwaita: list, grid and column views with models, item factories, sorting and filtering; header bars, toolbar views, tab views, notebooks and popover menus; menu sections and submenus; and composite-template signals.
* GTK designs also get a language service for `.ui` files, Blueprint (`.blp`) files through blueprint-compiler, and the project's own custom widgets running in the design host.
* A new Translations add-in edits gettext `.po` files in a translator Design view with language-service support, and translates a culture `.resx` against its neutral one.
* The designers now share their edit verbs and context-menu commands. The MewUI designer gains Paste Here, alignment and Grid track commands, the GTK designer gains align, grid and paste-here commands, and the WinUI designer mirrors the WPF designer's context menu.
* A failed designer build shows the compiler's real errors in the designer's status instead of a generic message.
* The Terminal finds PowerShell where Microsoft's installer puts it.
* Each download is now checked on its own platform before it is published: the Windows package on x64 and on ARM64, the macOS disk image after mounting it, and the Linux x64 and ARM64 archives after unpacking them.
